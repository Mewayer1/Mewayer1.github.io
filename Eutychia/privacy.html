隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

中文

Eutychia Strategic Advisory Limited十分重视访客的隐私保护。

1. 本网站本身不会主动收集访客的姓名、邮箱、电话等个人身份信息。网站不会部署追踪类Cookie用于采集用户个人数据。

2. 当访客通过邮件、电话等方式主动向我方提交联系方式、企业信息，仅用于业务沟通、项目对接。未经您的许可，不会向无关第三方泄露、出售您提交的个人与企业信息。

3. 仅在法律要求、司法机关依法要求的情形下，我方才会对外提供相关信息。

4. 如您希望删除联系过程中留存的您的相关信息，可以通过公开联系方式向我方提出申请。

English

Eutychia Strategic Advisory Limited respects visitor privacy.

1. This website does not actively collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address or phone number. We do not use tracking‑oriented cookies to gather visitor personal data.

2. When you voluntarily submit contact or enterprise information via email or telephone, such data will only be used for business communication and project‑related correspondence. Without your consent, we will not disclose or sell your personal or corporate information to unrelated third‑parties.

3. Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal orders from competent authorities.

4. You may submit a request via our public contact channels if you wish us to delete your retained information.
