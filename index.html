关于我们 About Us

Eutychia Strategic Advisory Limited 是由牛津等知名高校的年轻研究者组成的独立第三方专业咨询机构。无论是微观层面的细节研判，还是宏观维度的趋势分析，我们拥有足够能力串联多个独立领域，针对每一个棘手复杂的问题，为客户打造高度定制化的专业智库解决方案。 Eutychia Strategic Advisory Limited is an independent third‑party professional consulting institution composed of young researchers from renowned universities including Oxford. From micro‑level detailed judgment to macro‑dimensional trend analysis, we are capable of connecting multiple independent fields to deliver highly customized professional think‑tank solutions for challenging and complex client issues.


我们依托多元的学术背景与跨学科研究能力，打破领域壁垒，用严谨的研究视角，交付务实可行的咨询成果。 Leveraging diverse academic backgrounds and interdisciplinary research capabilities, we break disciplinary boundaries and deliver practical consulting outputs with rigorous research perspectives.

我们的服务模式 Our Service Model

我们并非传统意义上的咨询机构。多数咨询公司由客户发起需求再启动项目，而我们采用逆向的研究与交付逻辑。 We are not a traditional consulting firm. Most consulting projects start from client‑initiated demands, while we adopt a reversed research‑and‑delivery logic.

1

自主行业样本研究 Independent Industry Research

基于海量行业样本开展独立研究，挖掘行业普遍存在的结构性问题。 We conduct independent research based on massive industry samples to identify widespread structural problems.

2

推演并验证解法 Solution Derivation & Validation

站在第三方视角推导潜在解决路径，并对方案进行多轮合规、合法、合理性验证。 We derive potential solutions from a third‑party perspective and validate them repeatedly for compliance, legality and rationality.

3

拆解企业具体问题 Enterprise Problem Analysis

结合目标企业的现实处境，定向搜集关联信息，拆解企业面临的具体矛盾。 We analyze the real‑world context of target enterprises, collect relevant information and dissect their specific dilemmas.

4

构建多方共赢方案 Multi‑Party Win‑Win Solution

输出所有参与方都难以拒绝的务实方案。推导逻辑以打破存量博弈、长期整体利益为基石，完全不依赖人脉关系。 We deliver practical solutions difficult for all stakeholders to reject, based on breaking zero‑sum games, long‑term collective interests and no reliance on personal connections.

5

主动触达并交付建议 Proactive Outreach & Guidance

主动对接企业，阐明方案下如何实现收益最大化、损失最小化，并提供可落地的行动指引。 We pro‑actively approach enterprises, explain how to maximize benefits and minimize losses, and provide actionable guidance.
